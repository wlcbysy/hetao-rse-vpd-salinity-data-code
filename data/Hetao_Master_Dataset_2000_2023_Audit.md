## Interpretation Note

The local 2000-2020 yearly TIFF files contain LST, NIRv, Tmax, SM, and VPD, but do not contain GPP. The 2022 local GPP band is all zero and is excluded from response modeling as an invalid product layer. QC-passed MOD17A2H GPP values are retained for years with nonzero local GPP support and converted from scaled 8-day kg C m-2 composites to daily g C m-2 day-1 by multiplying by 125.0. Long-term risk projection should therefore use covariates such as VPD and NDSI, while GPP-response modeling should filter to QC-passed observed GPP rows.
